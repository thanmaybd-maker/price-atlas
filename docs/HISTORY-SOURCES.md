# Real history and free sources

Verified 29 October 2026. The retained Price Atlas database contains observations on October 6, 28 and 29. These are sparse checks, not a daily thirty-day series. A product first checked today cannot have seven earlier days fabricated by repeating its current price.

## What this release does

- Reads retained history in a single bounded batch. Without a watchlist, Home selects products with the most recorded days, rather than the newest imports with one check.
- Defaults Home to item prices, preserving useful observations when shipping is unknown. Delivered mode excludes unknown totals.
- Shows coverage, loading/error/retry, full product names, a weekly product selector and a daily-price table. Original check times and blank days remain intact.
- Provides outbound free tracker references and a copyable canonical product link. External observations do not enter our rankings or trigger our alerts.

## Sources investigated

| Source                                                                                                                                             | Access and scope                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [PriceHistory.app](https://pricehistory.app/)                                                                                                      | Public consumer charts are offered free; its [Business/API page](https://pricehistory.app/page/business) describes custom business services. Its [terms](https://pricehistory.app/page/tnc) restrict automated copying and redistribution without permission. | Provide its homepage for manual lookup. Do not scrape or repackage its dataset.                                                                                                              |
| [PriceHistory.ai FAQ](https://pricehistory.ai/faq)                                                                                                 | Public history pages can be viewed free without sign-in; newly tracked products may have limited history.                                                                                                                                                     | Provide a public reference link, without promising that every listing has older data. No undocumented data endpoint is used.                                                                 |
| [Keepa API](https://keepa.com/api-docs/)                                                                                                           | Programmatic histories and graph-image access use an API subscription and access key.                                                                                                                                                                         | Not a free backend replacement; no paid account or image-API dependency added.                                                                                                               |
| [ShopSavvy historical export](https://shopsavvy.com/data/documentation/recipe-price-history-export)                                                | Historical export is metered in credits per product and window.                                                                                                                                                                                               | Not introduced into this no-budget project.                                                                                                                                                  |
| [Open Prices API](https://prices.openfoodfacts.org/api/docs) and [license guidance](https://github.com/openfoodfacts/open-prices/blob/main/API.md) | Open product/receipt price observations, chiefly groceries, with ODbL attribution/share-alike requirements. Product barcode, currency, date and location identity matter.                                                                                     | A possible separate open grocery-data feature; not a valid substitute for Indian phone, lipstick or calculator histories. Do not merge unrelated products or foreign prices to fill a chart. |

An externally visible chart does not by itself grant a free data API or redistribution license. No source found establishes a free, unrestricted, exact-variant thirty-day history feed for all ten retailers. Ongoing primary-page collection is still necessary for our own future history; free collection remains subject to retailer availability and the existing shared request budget.
